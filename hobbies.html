I'm more than my tech stack. This page is a window into the things I love — stories that make me feel deeply, writing that keeps me grounded, and music that lives rent-free in my head.

Passion I

Reading

I read across genres — literary fiction, self-help, and quiet emotional novels that linger long after the last page. There's something about a story told with honesty that no algorithm can replicate.

My favourites are the kind of books that feel like a conversation — the ones where you finish a chapter and sit quietly for a moment before continuing. Telugu literature holds a very special place for me; reading in my mother tongue feels like coming home.

I also reach for self-help when I need direction or perspective. Not the hustle-culture kind — the kind that asks harder questions about who you want to become.

"A reader lives a thousand lives before she dies. The person who never reads lives only one."
— George R.R. Martin

Passion II

Writing

Reading so many novels made me want to write. I'm drawn to fiction and personal writing — capturing small moments, imagining other lives, putting words to feelings that don't have easy names.

Writing, for me, is also how I process things. A journal entry, a short story draft, a scene from a character's life — each one is a way of making sense of the world. I believe reading widely and writing honestly go hand in hand.

Fiction & Novels

Reading to Write Better

Every novel I read teaches me something about character, pacing, and emotion. Books like A Little Life and Almond showed me how much can be said with restraint — a lesson I try to carry into my own writing.

Personal Writing

Journaling & Reflection

I write privately — thoughts, observations, things that made me pause during the day. It's not for an audience. It's for clarity. Writing what I feel helps me understand what I actually think.

Creative

Short Fiction & Characters

I like writing small moments — a conversation, a memory, a turning point. Inspired by the emotional fiction I love reading. I'm interested in ordinary people in extraordinary inner worlds.

Passion III

Music

Music is always on. It follows me through study sessions, late-night coding, walks, and lazy afternoons. I don't stick to one genre — my playlist is a little bit of everything, and I love it that way.

Old Telugu songs are closest to my heart. There's a warmth and poetry in classic Telugu film music that nothing else quite matches — the kind of songs that carry entire memories in a single melody.

But I also live for a good mass number that hits hard, a pop song I can't stop humming, or a K-pop track with a chorus that's almost unfairly catchy. My music taste has no rules — only feelings.
